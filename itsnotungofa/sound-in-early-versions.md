## notes

sound is a good thing to want early, but it depends on how far the boot gets. other projects in this space (stela, liberator) have no working audio on macOS 27 yet either, so it is a hard problem for everyone right now.

on a pc, audio normally comes from the onboard hda chip (realtek and similar). macOS has no driver for most of them, so the hackintosh scene uses community drivers. that only becomes possible once the system boots and device drivers can load.
